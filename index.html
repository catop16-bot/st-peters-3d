教堂內部導覽

  • 雙擊教堂（或按「進入教堂內部」）經光之門進入。內部依真實尺寸建模：內部長 186.3 m、中殿拱頂 45.5 m、穹頂內徑 41.5 m、地面至燈籠亭內頂 117.57 m。
  • 47 張解說卡，涵蓋柱廊、中殿、側廊、交叉部、兩耳堂、後殿與兩座後殿轉角禮拜堂，包括 39 尊修會創辦人像；可選「精選」或依官方平面圖參觀順序的「完整路線」。每件展品都有自己的網址，可直接分享。
  • 修會創辦人像依官方位置圖分區擺放；同一區內各尊對應哪個壁龕，官方圖示無法辨識，因此雕像不逐一標名，改以分區名單呈現。
  • 解說依 stpetersbasilica.info 各展品頁（含所引梵蒂岡官方導覽文字）與 Wikipedia 撰寫，每張解說卡都附資料來源；各來源說法不一處以「一說」標註或不寫。
  • 展品位置依官方平面圖近似擺放。室內光束方向依外部當下的真實太陽位置計算；夜間改為暖色燈光。

About this model

This viewer rebuilds St. Peter's Basilica, St. Peter's Square and the surrounding Borgo district from real data. Nothing fictional is added.

Data sources

  • Building outlines and heights, roads, trees, street lamps and shop names: © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL 1.0)
  • Positions and names of the 140 colonnade statues and the 13 facade statues: OpenStreetMap
  • Key dimensions: facade 114.69 m wide and 45.55 m high; top of the dome cross 136.57 m; obelisk shaft 25.5 m (Wikipedia and other references)
  • Sun and moon positions: computed live for Rome's latitude and longitude and the chosen date and time (SunCalc)
  • Material textures: ambientCG (CC0)

Simplifications

  • Statues are simplified figures made for this site, posed and given their attributes from real photographs. They are not scans and cannot reproduce the carving in detail.
  • Surrounding facades are Roman-style elevations generated from OSM outlines and heights. Signs only use shop names that exist in OSM; shopfronts without data have no sign.
  • The ground is flat (the slope of the Vatican hill is not modelled).

Interior tour

  • Double-click the basilica (or press "Go inside") to pass through the Gate of Light. The interior is built at real scale: 186.3 m long inside, nave vault 45.5 m, dome 41.5 m across, 117.57 m from the floor to the lantern.
  • 47 cards cover the portico, nave, aisles, crossing, both transepts, the apse and the two apse corner chapels, including 39 statues of founder saints. Choose the highlights tour or the full route in the order of the official floor plan. Every exhibit has its own link you can share.
  • Founder statues are placed by section, following the official location map. Which niche holds which saint cannot be read from the official diagram, so the statues are not labelled one by one; each section's card lists its names instead.
  • Texts are based on the item pages of stpetersbasilica.info (including the Vatican guide texts they quote) and Wikipedia, and every card lists its sources. Where sources disagree, the card says so or leaves the detail out.
  • Exhibits are placed approximately according to the official floor plan. Interior light shafts follow the real sun position outside; at night the lighting turns warm.
